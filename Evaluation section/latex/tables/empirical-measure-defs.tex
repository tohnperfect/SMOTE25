    F-measure ($F_\beta$) & Composite & weights recall $\beta$ times as much as precision & $(1 + \beta^2)PR/(\beta^2 P + R)$ & 11\\

    Macro-F1 & Multi-class & F1 averaged over classes with equal weight & $\frac{1}{K}\sum_k F1_k$ & 7\\
    AvAcc & Multi-class & mean per-class recall (some studies use mean one-vs-rest accuracy, which true negatives inflate) & $\frac{1}{K}\sum_k r_k$ & 7\\
    Weighted F1 & Multi-class & F1 averaged with class-size weights; dominated by large classes & $\sum_k w_k F1_k$ & 1\\
    Macro-precision & Multi-class & precision averaged over classes & $\frac{1}{K}\sum_k P_k$ & 4\\
    Pmin & Multi-class & smallest per-class precision & $\min_k P_k$ & 1\\

    Minimum sensitivity & Multi-class & smallest per-class recall & $\min_k r_k$ & 2\\
